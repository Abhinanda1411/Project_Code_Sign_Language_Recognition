Every letter is one hand shape. Match the picture and the description, then hold the shape still for about half a second and the translator types the letter. Open the translator in another window to practise: the letter the camera sees lights up here.

Commands

Hold a letterKeep the shape still for about half a second.
Same letter twiceShow a relaxed hand (REST) between the two.
J and ZDraw them in the air, following the arrows below.
Clear the textHold both open palms up, fingers and thumbs spread.
Speak the textThumbs up, held for about a second.
Camera tipsOne hand, palm towards the camera, good light.

Letters

Pictures are drawn from real recordings: a stick hand as you see yourself in the camera (like a mirror). Dots closer to the camera are bigger. Colours: thumb orange, index blue, middle green, ring purple, little finger red. J and Z show the path of the fingertip (green dot = start).
